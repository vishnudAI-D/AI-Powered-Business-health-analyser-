🎯 5. Hackathon Judge Q&A Defense Sheet

Q1: Why use Google Gemini 2.5 Flash over a local open-source model?
Winning Answer: We optimized for sub-second voice latency (<400ms), native regional Indian dialect tokenization (Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Tanglish), and reliable JSON tool execution at near-zero API cost. Furthermore, our dual-path architecture automatically falls back to an embedded offline regex parser if internet is unavailable.
Q2: Why blended linear regression instead of an LSTM or Deep Learning model?
Winning Answer: MSME historical data is sparse (6–24 data points). Training deep neural networks or LSTMs on 12 points leads to severe overfitting. Our hybrid least-squares (55%) + moving average (45%) runs in <2ms, has zero cold-start delay, and provides interpretable confidence bounds business owners can trust.
Q3: Why would a shopkeeper use this instead of Tally or Excel?
Winning Answer: Tally and Excel are backward-looking accounting systems for tax compliance requiring manual entry in English. Vyapaar Pulse is a forward-looking decision copilot. Murugan simply speaks in Tamil: "How many sarees should I reorder?" and receives exact simulated quantities in seconds.
Q4: How do you handle mixed-language reviews like Tanglish/Hinglish with English VADER?
Winning Answer: We engineered a pre-tokenization dialect normalization layer in logic.py that translates colloquial regional tokens ("romba nalla", "ekdam bekaar") into calibrated valences before feeding into our 5-aspect categorization engine (Quality, Delivery, Packaging, Price, Support).
Q5: What is your monetization strategy?
Winning Answer: 3-tiered model: (1) Freemium MSME Tier at ₹499/mo, (2) Pro Merchant Tier at ₹1,499/mo with automated WhatsApp dispatch, and (3) B2B NBFC/Lender API for MSME credit-scoring to unlock faster collateral-free loans.
